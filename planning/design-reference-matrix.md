# Live design method comparison

Observed 2026-11-18; methods only, no copied code, art or copy.

| Site | Adopt | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| suzysays.org | Short factual entry and clear next-task links | Dense navigation, early ending exposure and numerous thin adjacent topics |
| suzysaysgame.com | Player-first position and nearby next-game choices | Online claim that obscures original Windows release, overwhelming catalog navigation and unrelated broad recommendations |
| itch.io/games/tag-horror | Recognizable covers, compact repeatable cards and clear game identity | Faceted marketplace density, account controls and sale emphasis unnecessary for eight games |

## Composition alternatives
1. Bright DVD booklet: light paper, colorful command tiles and editorial columns. Readable but loses dark game-frame continuity and makes many covers compete.
2. CRT room: dark ink surfaces, warm yellow activation control, restrained violet borders and four small mat-colored accents. Selected: the player remains the brightest media surface; the long article has clear chapter rhythm; compact side covers remain individually recognizable.

The selected direction uses an original terminal/CRT logo, inset player rails and short chapter-rule accents in Header, player, article and Footer. No generic gaming stock image or recoloring of real covers.
